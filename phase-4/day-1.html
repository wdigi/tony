Phase 4 · Day 1

3D basics

The ideas behind every 3D tool: shapes, units, axes and the two kinds of 3D.

Learn

  • Axes: X left–right, Y front–back, Z up (in most CAD tools).
  • Units: DiGiKits works in millimetres at real scale. A 1 m desk is 1000 mm in the model.
  • CAD (solid) models are exact shapes for making real parts: enclosures, brackets.
  • Meshes are surfaces of tiny triangles, used for showing things on screen: showrooms, games.
  • File types: STEP (exact CAD), STL (mesh, for 3D printing), GLB/glTF (mesh with colours, for the web).

Practice

Install FreeCAD (freecad.org). Open a sample file, orbit, pan and zoom, and measure two parts.

Done when

  • You can explain CAD vs mesh and when each is used
  • You can move around a model comfortably
