Phase 4 · Day 2

FreeCAD: sketch and pad

Make your first real part: a plate with holes, from a sketch.

Steps

  1. Part Design workbench → Create body → Create sketch on the XY plane.
  2. Draw a rectangle 100 × 60 mm and four circles 3.2 mm across, 5 mm in from each corner. Add dimensions so the sketch turns fully constrained (it changes colour).
  3. Close the sketch and Pad it 3 mm.
  4. Add a Fillet of 2 mm to the four corner edges.
  5. Export as STEP and as STL.

Done when

  • A fully constrained plate with holes, exported in two formats
