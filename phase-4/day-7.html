Phase 4 · Day 7

3D on the web, part 2

Load a real model, let the viewer orbit around it, and show it on the Big Screen.

Practice

  1. Export your enclosure as a mesh and convert it to GLB (FreeCAD or Blender can do this).
  2. Load it with Three.js GLTFLoader, and add OrbitControls so the mouse or a touch screen can turn it.
  3. Add a button that switches a light on and off in the scene.
  4. Publish the page (GitHub Pages works) and show it on your Phase 2 Big Screen in kiosk mode.

Done when

  • Your own model turns in the browser and on the Big Screen
