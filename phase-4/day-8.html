Examples

  • A 3D scene for a training or driving simulation.
  • A small 3D showroom: a room where you can switch products and lights.
  • A script-driven 3D concept of a machine or desk before parts are bought.

Write a one-page brief

Project name:
What it shows or does (two sentences):
Who uses it:
Done when (three checks):
First milestone (one week):

Cut it down to a first version

An example brief

Project name:  Showroom light switcher
What it does:  A 3D room on the Big Screen where a visitor taps to switch
               between three lighting moods on a model kitchen.
Who uses it:   Showroom visitors; DiGiKits as a sales demo.
Done when:     1. Runs full screen on the Pi kiosk, loads in under 10 s
               2. Three moods, switched by touch, change in under 1 s
               3. README: how to run it, how to add a mood
First milestone (one week):
               One room, one model, two moods, switching by a button on screen.

Questions your coach will ask

  • Who uses it, and what do they do with it in the first 10 seconds?
  • What's the riskiest part? (Do that first.)
  • What will you show at the end of week one?
  • Does it help DiGiKits show or sell something?

Done when

  • Brief agreed with your coach and saved in a new GitHub repo
  • Your feature list is sorted into Must, Nice and Later
  • Your brief names the riskiest part and a week-one milestone
