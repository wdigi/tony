Phase 4 · Day 3

FreeCAD: an enclosure

Design a simple box for a Raspberry Pi, the kind of part DiGiKits products need.

📝Update progress on the message board below ↓

Steps

  1. Measure your Pi (or look up its official drawing). Note the board size and the mounting hole positions.
  2. Pad a base, then use Pocket to hollow it out, leaving 2 mm walls.
  3. Add four standoffs at the Pi's hole positions.
  4. Cut openings for the power and HDMI ports.
  5. Change one dimension (the wall thickness) and watch the whole model update.

The Pi 5's numbers

Rules for a box that works

  • Clearance: about 0.5 mm around the board, more near connectors so plugs fit.
  • Walls 2 mm for 3D printing; thinner breaks, thicker wastes time.
  • Standoffs about 3 mm tall so solder points under the board don't touch the floor. Holes sized for the screw or a heat-set insert.
  • Heat: the Pi 5 gets hot. Vents low on one side and high on the other let air flow through, and the cooler needs space above it.
  • Access: can you still reach the SD card, the power button and the GPIO header for a cable?
  • Lid: a lip with about 0.2–0.3 mm gap per side for a snug fit.

Put a small version number in the model (for example "v3" embossed inside the lid). When a print comes back, you know which design it is.

Done when

  • An enclosure that fits the Pi's real measurements
  • Changing one number updates the model correctly
  • Your enclosure uses the real hole spacing and has clearance
  • It has vents and access to the SD card
  • You did the five-question review and noted the result

More cases

Try each one before you open the answer.

The Pi fits, but the HDMI plug won't go in.

The opening was sized for the socket, not the plug's body. Make connector openings bigger than the socket, and test with the real cable.

The Pi throttles after 10 minutes in the box.

Too hot. Check vcgencmd get_throttled; add vents (low in, high out) and space for the cooler, then test again for 30 minutes.

Screws split the standoffs.

Holes too small or walls too thin around them. Make the standoff thicker, or design for heat-set inserts.
