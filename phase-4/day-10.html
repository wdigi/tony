Phase 4 · Day 10

Project: show it

Finish your project's first version and present it.

📝Update progress on the message board below ↓

The check

  • It runs on a screen (the Big Screen or a laptop) with no setup during the demo.
  • The repo has a README: what it is, how to run it, what's next.
  • A 5-minute demo to your coach: what it does, how you built it, what you'd do next.

A five-minute demo

Have a plan B

  • Start the demo before your coach arrives, already loaded.
  • Keep a local copy that runs without internet.
  • Record a 1-minute screen video the day before. If anything fails, show the video and carry on.

README template

With Claude: rehearse with a tough audience

Here's my project README and my 5-minute demo plan [attached].
1. Point out anything a newcomer couldn't follow in the README.
2. Play a busy client watching my demo: ask me the 5 hardest questions, one at a time,
   and tell me after each answer how to say it more clearly.

Then ask Claude to turn your "what's next" list into GitHub issues with clear done-when lines, ready for the next round.

Done when

  • Demo given; next steps agreed
  • You rehearsed the demo with a timer
  • A 1-minute video of the project is saved
  • The README passes the 5-minute test with someone new
  • Claude played a tough audience and you improved three answers

From here the track is open-ended: your coach and you plan the next milestones together.

More cases

Try each one before you open the answer.

The Wi-Fi fails right before the demo.

Plan B: the local copy, or the video. Say what happened in one sentence and carry on calmly; that's a skill too.

You run out of time at minute 3.

You started with the running thing, so the important part is already shown. Skip to “what's next” and finish on time.
