Phase 1 · Day 2

What AI is, and first boot

Morning: what AI is and why it sits at the centre of DiGiKits. Afternoon: put an operating system on a blank card and get the Pi running.

Part A · What AI is (morning)

Read "An Everyday Way to Understand How AI Works" (your coach sends you the link). Here are its main ideas.

The firm that forgets

Picture a firm full of brilliant experts: lawyers, engineers, designers, writers. They work 24 hours a day and never get tired. The catch: none of them remember you. Every visit starts with "How can we help you today?"

Behind all the office doors there is really one mind wearing different hats. That mind is the AI model. Each conversation is a fresh shift with no memory of yesterday.

At the firmIn AIWhat it means
The expert's educationThe modelEverything it learned in training
A client meetingA conversationStarts fresh, ends as one session
The client file on the deskThe contextEverything the AI knows right now
The receptionistThe software around the AIPicks which file and which pages to hand over

Four things to remember

  1. AI memory is reading, not remembering. The system slips your file onto the desk before each conversation. It's an open-book exam.
  2. You pay for what the AI reads. Hand it the whole filing cabinet for a small question and the bill is big. Small job, small file.
  3. Talk to it like a person, check it like a machine. Be clear, give examples, give feedback. But AI can sound confident while guessing, so verify its work.
  4. Be ambitious, with boundaries. Ask for big work, but say what must not change: "Make it faster without reducing quality."

Why AI is the centre of DiGiKits

DiGiKits is a Physical AI company: AI working with real machines, lights and boards for small businesses.

  • The lab is run by AI. DigitLab, an AI, runs DiGiKits Lab: it schedules tests, runs scripts and talks to visitors.
  • Design is AI-assisted. Customers describe a board and the design system builds it, with an AI review loop.
  • Customers get AI helpers. Many support questions are answered first by an AI, using the knowledge base your support notes feed.
  • The office runs on AI agents. Much of DiGiKits' own daily work is done by AI agents that people direct and review.
  • DiGiKits builds receptionists. The real product is the software that hands AI the right information about a customer's machines, floors and people.

Your place in this: AI knows patterns; it has never touched a hot motor. When AI runs a machine, someone has to bring the hands-on knowledge, check its work and keep the hardware healthy. That's the support role you are training for.

Try it

Set up the AI chat sidebar (see Tools) on your laptop now, or on the Pi this afternoon. Ask it the same thing twice: once vaguely ("explain Linux"), once with a clear goal and limits ("explain Linux to a beginner in 5 bullet points, no jargon"). Compare the answers.

Part B · First boot (afternoon)

Every new DiGiKits device starts here. Support often means re-flashing a card.

Steps

  1. On your laptop, install Raspberry Pi Imager from raspberrypi.com/software.
  2. Choose device: Raspberry Pi 5. OS: Raspberry Pi OS (64-bit). Storage: your SD card.
  3. When asked, edit settings: set a hostname (e.g. trainpi), a username and password, your Wi-Fi, and your time zone. On the Services tab, turn on SSH.
  4. Write the card, put it in the Pi, connect monitor, keyboard, mouse, then power last.
  5. Open Terminal and update everything: 
    sudo apt update
sudo apt full-upgrade -y
sudo reboot

Done when

  • You can explain the firm, the client file and the receptionist in your own words
  • You can give two reasons AI is the centre of DiGiKits, and where your job fits
  • The Pi boots to the desktop
  • The update finishes with no errors
  • You re-flash the card from scratch 3 times; the last one takes under 20 minutes

Support drill

Your coach gives you a card with the wrong Wi-Fi password. Find out why the Pi has no internet and fix it. Write a support note (template).
