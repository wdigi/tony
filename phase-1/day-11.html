Phase 1 · Day 11

Final check

Show you can do it alone: revive a broken Pi with no hints.

Morning: final check

You get a Pi that doesn't work. Use everything from days 2–10: power, card, network, SSH, permissions, services, logs. Get it running and explain the fault.

Afternoon: tidy up

  • All support notes from the two weeks written up with the template
  • Your own cheat sheet of the commands you used most
  • A list of what still feels shaky, to go over with your coach

Pass the check and Phase 2 opens.
