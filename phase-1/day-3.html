Phase 1 · Day 3

Remote access

Control the Pi from your laptop or phone, at home and from anywhere.

Why it matters

Most DiGiKits support is remote. SSH is the main tool.

Steps

  1. SSH on? If you didn't set it in Imager: sudo raspi-config → Interface Options → SSH → Yes.
  2. From your laptop (same Wi-Fi): ssh yourname@trainpi.local
  3. Use a key instead of a password. On your laptop: 
    ssh-keygen -t ed25519
ssh-copy-id yourname@trainpi.local
  4. Reach it from outside the house with Tailscale. On the Pi: 
    curl -fsSL https://tailscale.com/install.sh | sh
sudo tailscale up
    Open the link it prints and sign in. Install Tailscale on your laptop or phone with the same account.
  5. Now SSH by its Tailscale name from anywhere: ssh yourname@trainpi

Done when

  • You log in with your key, no password asked
  • You log in from your phone on mobile data, not home Wi-Fi

Support drill

SSH suddenly refuses to connect. Find out why (hint: is the service running? is the name right?).
