Phase 1 · Day 1

First boot

Put an operating system on a blank card and get the Pi running and up to date.

Why it matters

Every new DiGiKits device starts here. Support often means re-flashing a card.

Steps

  1. On your laptop, install Raspberry Pi Imager from raspberrypi.com/software.
  2. Choose device: Raspberry Pi 5. OS: Raspberry Pi OS (64-bit). Storage: your SD card.
  3. When asked, edit settings: set a hostname (e.g. trainpi), a username and password, your Wi-Fi, and your time zone. On the Services tab, turn on SSH.
  4. Write the card, put it in the Pi, connect monitor, keyboard, mouse, then power last.
  5. Open Terminal and update everything: 
    sudo apt update
sudo apt full-upgrade -y
sudo reboot

Done when

  • The Pi boots to the desktop
  • The update finishes with no errors
  • You re-flash the card from scratch 3 times; the last one takes under 20 minutes

Support drill

Your coach gives you a card with the wrong Wi-Fi password. Find out why the Pi has no internet and fix it. Write a support note (template).
