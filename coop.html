Your first project

Your first real job is an internal dashboard: a web page that shows live status and numbers from one part of the business at a glance. It's a good first project because it touches everything in your path: data, a small server, Docker, the pipeline, and a person who uses it every day and tells you what's wrong.

Which dashboard, its data and its users are explained by your coach when you start; they're internal, so they're not on this page.

How you work day to day

  • Small tasks. Every piece of work starts as a small GitHub issue.
  • Branch, pull request, review. You never push straight to main; the pipeline checks your work and a person reviews it.
  • Claude as teammate. Claude drafts with you; you test, decide what goes in, and own the result (Lesson 8).
  • Write it down. Every fix and decision ends up in a README, a guide or the knowledge base.
  • Check in. A short regular check-in with your coach: what you did, what's next, what's blocking you.

Co-op goals

  • I finished the Physical AI Systems series and passed its gate
  • I finished the Linux, Git and Docker lessons on my path
  • I finished the deploy and pipeline sessions
  • My first pull request was reviewed and merged
  • My first project is live and someone uses it
  • I handled a support case with my coach watching
  • I wrote one knowledge-base article or guide
  • I can explain the main DiGiKits products and the hardware behind them
