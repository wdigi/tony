DiGiKits Technical Support Training

Learn to set up, check and fix the Raspberry Pi computers, boards and software behind DiGiKits products. Mostly from home, at your own pace.

Start here: Phase 1, Week 1 — First boot. You need the starter kit first.

How this works

  • Your pace. Your coach helps; you decide the speed.
  • Short sessions: about 1 hour a day, 4–5 days a week. A missed week is fine.
  • Learn by doing: every week ends with something that works, not a quiz.
  • One support drill a week: something is broken on purpose and you find the fault.

Roadmap

Each phase opens when you pass the gate before it. Weeks are estimates, not deadlines.
