DiGiKits Technical Support Training

Learn to set up, check and fix the Raspberry Pi computers, boards and software behind DiGiKits products. Mostly from home, at your own pace.

Start here: Phase 1, Day 1 — First boot. You need the starter kit first.

How this works

  • Your pace. Your coach helps; you decide the speed.
  • Full days: 6 hours a day, 5 days a week. Weekends off.
  • Learn by doing: every day ends with something that works, not a quiz.
  • A support drill every day: something is broken on purpose and you find the fault.

Daily rhythm

TimeBlock
9:00–10:30Learn: read the day's lesson, follow along on the Pi
10:45–12:00Practice: repeat the steps without the guide
13:00–14:30Build: a small task using today's skill
14:45–15:45Support drill, support note, short check-in with your coach
