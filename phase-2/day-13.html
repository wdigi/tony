Phase 2 · Day 13

Monitoring and alerts

Know a system is in trouble before the customer calls.

Why it matters

Good support is mostly invisible: problems fixed before anyone notices.

Practice

  1. In Uptime Kuma (Day 9), add monitors for every Pi (ping), every web page (HTTP) and the fake controller (TCP port).
  2. Set up a notification to your phone (Kuma supports many apps; pick one your coach agrees on).
  3. Extend your health script: warn when temperature is over 75 °C or the power check isn't 0x0.
  4. Keep logs small: check /etc/logrotate.d/ and add a rule for health.log.

Done when

  • Unplugging a Pi sends an alert to your phone within 2 minutes
  • Your health log rotates instead of growing forever

Support drill

Too many alerts at night for a Pi that's fine. Tune the monitor so it alerts only when it matters.
