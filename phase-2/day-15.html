Phase 2 · Day 15

Phase 2 gate

Show you can set up a real DiGiKits-style system on your own.

The check

  1. Start from a blank SD card and follow only your own written guide from Day 11.
  2. Build a Big Screen: kiosk page on boot, TV on/off by schedule, mode button.
  3. Run your fake LED controller as a service, and drive a real LED strip from the Pico.
  4. Add everything to monitoring with phone alerts.
  5. Fix your guide wherever you had to think.

Done when

  • Everything works after a power cut, with no keyboard attached
  • Your coach signs off; Phase 3 opens
