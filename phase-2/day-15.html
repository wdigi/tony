Phase 2 · Day 15

Phase 2 gate

Show you can set up a real DiGiKits-style system on your own.

📝Update progress on the message board below ↓

The check

  1. Start from a blank SD card and follow only your own written guide from Day 11.
  2. Build a Big Screen: kiosk page on boot, TV on/off by schedule, mode button.
  3. Run your fake LED controller as a service, and drive a real LED strip from the Pico.
  4. Add everything to monitoring with phone alerts.
  5. Fix your guide wherever you had to think.

The gate day, hour by hour

Post your evidence

On this lesson's message board, post: a link to your guide's pull request, a screenshot of Kuma all green, and one photo of the setup (only our equipment). Your coach replies there with the result.

Where people usually get stuck

  • The kiosk starts before the network (Day 3).
  • Timers in the wrong time zone (Day 2).
  • The service uses the wrong Python (Phase 1 Day 9).
  • The strip shares the Pico's power (Day 7).
  • Alerts that never reach the phone because the notification was never tested (Day 13).

Done when

  • Everything works after a power cut, with no keyboard attached
  • Your coach signs off; Phase 3 opens
  • Your guide's fixes are merged through a pull request
  • You posted your evidence on the board
  • You walked your coach through the system in 10 minutes

Warm-up cases

Try each one before you open the answer.

After the power-cut test, everything is back except the TV.

The tv-on timer only fires at 08:55. After a power cut mid-day, nothing turns the TV on. Decide with your coach: run tv-on at boot during opening hours too, or accept it and document it.

Kuma alerted, but your phone stayed silent.

Test the notification from Kuma's settings page. Check the phone app's own notification settings and Do Not Disturb.
