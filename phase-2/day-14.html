Phase 2 · Day 14

Safety and the limits of the job

Know what you may fix yourself, and when to stop.

Why it matters

DiGiKits makes commercial-grade products. A careless fix can damage equipment, void a warranty or hurt someone.

Learn

  • Low voltage only. You work on 5–24 V circuits. Mains wiring and power supply internals are for licensed electricians.
  • Power off before wiring. Every time.
  • Static (ESD): touch a grounded metal surface or wear a wrist strap before handling bare boards.
  • Heat and smell: a hot wire or burning smell means power off now, then investigate.
  • Customer sites: touch only what you were asked to, photograph before and after, leave it tidy.
  • When to stop: anything involving mains, water, ladders above your comfort, or a fix you can't undo, call your coach first.

Practice

Write your own one-page safety checklist for a site visit and a lab day. Your coach reviews it.

Done when

  • Your safety checklist is approved
  • You can say which jobs are not yours to do

Support drill

Your coach describes three situations. For each, say: fix it, or stop and call.
