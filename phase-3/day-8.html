Phase 3 · Day 8

Escalation and incidents

Know when a problem is bigger than you, and what to do in the first ten minutes.

Escalate when

  • A customer's system is fully down during business hours and you can't fix it in 30 minutes.
  • Anything involving safety, mains power, smoke or heat.
  • Possible security problem: unknown logins, changed passwords, strange traffic.
  • Data might be lost, or you're about to do something you can't undo.

First ten minutes of an incident

  1. Make it safe (power off if there's any heat or smell).
  2. Tell your coach and the customer: what's affected, what you know, when the next update comes.
  3. Start a timeline log right away.
  4. Restore service first (a spare unit, a restart, a backup), find the root cause after.

After an incident

Write a short review: what happened, timeline, cause, what fixed it, what changes so it's caught faster next time. No blame.

Practice

Your coach runs a surprise incident drill. Handle it, then write the review.

Done when

  • Drill handled with updates on time
  • Review written and approved
