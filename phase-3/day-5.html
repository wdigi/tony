Phase 3 · Day 5

The knowledge base

Turn your support notes into articles that answer the next customer, or the AI helper, before anyone has to call.

📝Update progress on the message board below ↓

Why it matters

DiGiKits AI helpers answer customers from the knowledge base. Clear articles mean better AI answers and fewer tickets.

An article holds

Title:     the problem in the customer's words ("Screen is dark after a power cut")
Applies to: product and version
Symptoms:  what the customer sees
Cause:     one or two sentences
Fix:       numbered steps a customer can follow
If that didn't work: what to send support

Practice

Turn five of your best support notes from Phases 1–2 into articles in a GitHub repo. Ask the AI sidebar to read one and answer a customer question from it; fix the article where the AI's answer was wrong.

Four kinds of article

Write for how people search

  • Title in the customer's words: "Screen is black", not "HDMI-CEC input selection failure".
  • An "Also called" line with other words people use: blank, dark, nothing on the TV, no picture. Both people and the AI find it more easily.
  • One problem per article. Two problems in one article confuse the AI's answer.

Customer-safe and internal

Customer articles have steps a customer can do: check a plug, press a button, restart from the menu. Steps that need SSH, passwords, IP addresses or our internal names go in a separate internal article for support staff, never in the public one.

A complete example

Screen is black after a power cut
Also called: blank screen, no picture, TV shows nothing · Applies to: DiGiKits Big Screen, 2026 version · Last checked: Nov 2026
Symptoms: After a power cut, the TV stays black or shows "No signal".
Cause: The TV came back on a different input before the screen computer was ready.
Fix: 1. Press the TV remote's Input (or Source) button. 2. Choose HDMI 1. 3. Wait 30 seconds: the page appears.
Still black? Contact support with a photo of the screen and the time the power came back.

Done when

  • Five articles published in the repo
  • The AI gives a correct answer from each one
  • Each of your articles has a type, an “Also called” line and a “Last checked” date
  • You split one article into a customer version and an internal version
  • You fixed an article after the AI answered wrongly from it

More cases

Try each one before you open the answer.

A customer article contains ssh tony@192.168.50.50.

Internal details in a public article. Move those steps to an internal article; leave customer-safe steps and “contact support” in the public one.

The AI gave two different answers to the same question.

Probably two articles that disagree. Find both, merge them into one correct article, and retire the other.

Customers keep opening tickets even though an article exists.

They aren't finding it, or can't follow it. Check the title uses their words, add an “Also called” line, and test the steps with someone non-technical.
