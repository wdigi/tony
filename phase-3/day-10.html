Phase 3 · Day 10

Phase 3 gate

Handle 5 real support cases with your coach watching, start to finish.

The check

  • Every case: correct severity, replies on time, a clear log, closed with the customer's confirmation.
  • At least one case fixed remotely, by the rules.
  • At least one knowledge-base article written or improved from these cases.

Done when

  • Five cases handled; your coach signs off
  • You're ready to take support cases on your own

Congratulations: this is the goal of the training. Phase 4 is your own track.
