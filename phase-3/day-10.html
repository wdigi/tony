Phase 3 · Day 10

Phase 3 gate

Handle 5 real support cases with your coach watching, start to finish.

📝Update progress on the message board below ↓

The check

  • Every case: correct severity, replies on time, a clear log, closed with the customer's confirmation.
  • At least one case fixed remotely, by the rules.
  • At least one knowledge-base article written or improved from these cases.

How the gate is scored

Evidence to post

On this lesson's board, list the five ticket numbers, the knowledge-base article you wrote or improved (link), and one thing you learned from each case. No customer names or details: ticket numbers are enough. Your coach replies with the result.

After the gate

  • You take support cases on your own, with your coach available for escalations.
  • For the first month, your coach reads your closed tickets each Friday and gives feedback.
  • Phase 4 starts alongside: your own track.

Done when

  • Five cases handled; your coach signs off
  • You're ready to take support cases on your own
  • Your evidence is posted on the board (ticket numbers, article link, lessons)

Congratulations: this is the goal of the training. Phase 4 is your own track.

Warm-up cases

Try each one before you open the answer.

Case 4 turns out to need a site visit with mains work.

Escalate and hand it over correctly; that still counts. Knowing when to stop is part of the gate.

A case is fixed but the customer hasn't confirmed by end of day.

It stays “Fixed, waiting on customer”. Follow up the next morning; close only after they confirm (or after the agreed waiting time).
