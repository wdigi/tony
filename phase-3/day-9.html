Phase 3 · Day 9

Shadow cases

Handle real support cases with your coach beside you.

📝Update progress on the message board below ↓

How it works

  1. Take the next real case. Your coach watches and only steps in if something could go wrong.
  2. Follow the full flow: ticket, diagnose, fix or escalate, reply, close, note.
  3. After each case, a 5-minute review: what went well, one thing to do better.

Before you pick up a real case

  • Agree a signal word with your coach: when you say it, they step in, no questions. Using it is a good call, not a failure.
  • Have your tools ready: the ticket system, your session log (script), the knowledge base, your cheat sheet.
  • Read the customer's past tickets: has this happened before? What did we do?

What your coach is watching

AreaGood looks like
TicketSeverity right, steps with times, next update set
MethodLikely and easy checks first, one change at a time
SafetyBackups before edits, permission before access, no secrets pasted
CustomerPlain words, on-time updates, clear close
KnowledgeSupport note written; article created or improved if needed

When you don't know

You will meet things you haven't learned. Say so honestly and give a time: "I'll check that and get back to you by 3 p.m." Then use the knowledge base, Claude (with clean logs) and your coach, in that order.

Done when

  • Two or three real cases handled and closed
  • Each has a ticket, replies and a support note
  • You and your coach agreed a signal word
  • Each case has a 5-minute review written down

More cases

Try each one before you open the answer.

Your coach says the signal word in the middle of a session.

Stop typing, hands off. Listen. Afterwards, ask what they saw; it's the most useful lesson of the day.

The customer's past tickets show the same fault three times this year.

Mention it to your coach: the real fix may be bigger than this ticket (a part to replace, a check to add, an article to write).
